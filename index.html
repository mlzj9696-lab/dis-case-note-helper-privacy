Privacy Policy

DIS Case Note Helper (Chrome extension) — Last updated: 03 October 2026

DIS Case Note Helper is a Chrome extension built for clinic staff to insert saved clinical-note phrases and text shortcuts into note fields on dis.moh.gov.my. This page explains what data the extension handles and, just as importantly, what it does not do.

In short: the extension does not send any of your data to the developer or to any third-party server. Everything it stores stays inside your own Google/Chrome account, using Chrome's built-in storage.

What is stored, and where

The extension saves the following, so your setup is available whenever you open the panel:

  • Your saved phrases and text shortcuts — the clinical note snippets you add or edit. These are saved with Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API, which is Google's own account-sync storage. This is what lets the same phrases appear on your other devices when you're signed into the same Chrome profile.
  • Your trigger prefix for shortcuts (e.g. /) — also saved via chrome.storage.sync.
  • On/off state for shortcuts, and your login-autofill fields (Negeri, Daerah, Klinik, and saved Nama Pengguna / Nama Pembantu entries) — saved locally on your device only, via chrome.storage.local. These are not synced across devices.

All of this data is written and read only by the extension itself, through Chrome's own storage APIs. None of it passes through, or is visible to, the developer.

What the extension does on web pages

The extension only runs on pages under moh.gov.my (specifically dis.moh.gov.my). On those pages it:

  • Inserts the phrase or shortcut text you select into the currently focused note field.
  • Optionally fills in login fields (Negeri, Daerah, Klinik, Kata Laluan, Nama Pengguna, Nama Pembantu) that you've chosen to save, if you've turned autofill on.
  • With the optional "Auto Note" feature, reads the treatment/charge table (Jadual Caj), treatment plan (Pelan Rawatan), and tooth-chart markings already displayed on the page, and uses them — together with your own saved shortcuts and templates — to draft the clinical note text for you to review before saving.

All of this happens locally in your browser. The extension does not transmit any page content, patient data, or clinical information to the developer or to any external server.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, sell, or transfer personal data, health information, financial information, credentials, browsing history, or website content to the developer or any third party.
  • It does not use any remote/third-party servers, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not run any code that isn't included in the extension package itself (no remote scripts, no eval).

Your saved password field

The optional "Kata Laluan" (password) field used for login autofill is saved only in chrome.storage.local on your own device, for your own convenience. It is never synced, never transmitted anywhere by the extension, and is only ever read back to fill the login form on dis.moh.gov.my.

Uninstalling

Removing the extension from Chrome deletes its local storage. Data held in chrome.storage.sync is cleared from Google's sync storage for this extension as well, following Chrome's standard behavior for synced extension data.
